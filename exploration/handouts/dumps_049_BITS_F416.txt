1. Course Description:
Nanotechnology is widely regarded as a transformative field with the potential to address 
critical challenges in energy, healthcare, electronics, and advanced manufacturing. This 
course is designed to introduce students to the fundamental concepts of nanoscience and 
nanotechnology, preparing them for further academic research or industrial careers in this 
rapidly evolving area. The course focuses on the fundamental science behind nanomaterials, 
their synthesis and properties of nanomaterials, with emphasis on their size-dependent 
physical, chemical, electrical, optical, and thermal properties. Applications of nanomaterials 
in electronics, medicine, MEMS, photonics, catalysis, energy systems, and heat transfer will 
be discussed. The course also introduces essential quantum mechanical concepts relevant to 
nanoscale systems. In addition, emerging trends, large-scale manufacturing challenges, and 
future directions in nanoscience and nanotechnology are covered.
2. Scope and Objective of the Course:
The course is structured to provide a comprehensive and interdisciplinary overview 
of nanoscience and nanotechnology. Students will learn common methods for the synthesis 
of nanoparticles and nanostructures.
Special emphasis is placed on understanding the unique properties of nanomaterials 
and the scientific principles governing their behavior at the nanoscale. The course highlights 
real-world motivations and applications of nanomaterials across multiple technological 
domains.
This course is well suited for students seeking foundational knowledge and practical 
exposure to nanoscience and nanotechnology, and serves as an effective entry point into 
advanced study or research in the field.
